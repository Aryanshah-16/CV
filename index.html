Academic, Research and Goals Statement

Background

Through VEX robotics, I design, build, and solve problems hands-on, combining my interests in engineering and teamwork. Outside of school, I enjoy playing pickleball and badminton. Traveling to Paris and attending a research conference with my teammates introduced me to people who share my curiosity about research and shaped how I want to learn and collaborate. I am also involved in research on lung cancer and genetic engineering with E. coli.

Statement

I learn best by building things, which is why I plan to study aeronautical engineering. I hope to take on hands-on projects, including work with aircraft, and apply what I learn in the classroom. Research is also important to me. I plan to pursue wet-lab research in genetic engineering, including work with E. coli, and contribute to research on lung and breast cancer involving copper compounds. I also hope to present my work at conferences, where I can share ideas and learn from other researchers.

In the long term, I hope to work in aircraft manufacturing or maintenance, either in the commercial aviation industry or in military aviation. I am interested in opportunities with companies such as Boeing, Airbus, or Lockheed Martin, and I may also pursue Air Force service through ROTC. Whatever path I take, I want to help build or maintain safe, reliable aircraft.
