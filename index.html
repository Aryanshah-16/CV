Academic, Research and Goals Statement

Background

I am a student involved in VEX robotics, where I design, build, and solve problems hands-on. It fits perfectly with my love of engineering. Outside of academics, I spend my free time playing pickleball and badminton. Both keep me active, focused and ready to adapt quickly. I have also traveled to Paris twice, and it taught me that I love meeting and talking with new people. One week there with teammates at a research conference stood out the most. I met other people who were just as curious about research as I am, and that experience shaped how I want to learn and work in the future. I also am very involved in research in lung cancer and engineering E-Coli.

Statement

I learn best by building things, which is why I want to become an aeronautical engineer. My academic goal is to study aeronautical engineering and take on hands-on projects, especially work on real planes, so I can turn what I learn in class into skills I can use. Research is also important to me. I plan to conduct wet-lab research in genetic engineering, including work with E. coli, and contribute to multiple research papers, including studies on lung cancer and breast cancer that use copper compounds. I also want to attend and present at numerous conferences, because I love talking with people about my research and hearing about theirs.

My long-term goal is to work in manufacturing or repair for major airliners or the military. I would like to work for a company such as Boeing, Airbus in Europe, or a local military contractor like Lockheed Martin, or join the Air Force through ROTC in college. I would also be proud to work in airliner maintenance for airlines such as Delta, Air France, or Lufthansa, keeping aircraft safe for the people who fly on them.
